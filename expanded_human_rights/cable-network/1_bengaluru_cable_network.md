### Electromagnetic Propulsion Mechanism

**Tower-Based Acceleration System:**
- **Electromagnetic coils** in towers launch carriers using 5-50 kW variable power
- **15-meter acceleration zone** per tower with progressive coil activation  
- **Contactless magnetic coupling** - no electrical contacts or wear parts
- **400-1200m tower spacing** depending on load category - momentum carries between towers
- **Regenerative braking** captures energy during deceleration and descent

**Safety & Reliability:**
- **Cables NOT electrified** - remain at ground potential for safety
- **Magnetic induction** only in 15m tower zones when carriers present
- **Emergency mechanical brakes** engage automatically if power fails
- **Weather immune** - no electrical systems on cables themselves
