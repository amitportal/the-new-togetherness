## Core Innovation: Distributed Tower Propulsion System

### Fundamental Design Philosophy
Instead of heavy onboard motors, we use **distributed electromagnetic launchers** at support towers that accelerate ultra-lightweight carriers along the cable. This eliminates 90% of moving deadweight while achieving higher speeds and lower energy consumption.
